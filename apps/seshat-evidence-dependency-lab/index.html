MECHANISM DEMONSTRATION / NOT A RANKING

Dependency counts and ablations describe this bounded public export. They do not rank polities, evidence regimes, source quality, or historical truth.

These three polities are illustrative fixtures selected for contrasting source regimes. They are not a statistical sample and are not used to infer a general relationship between historical documentation and epistemic robustness.
