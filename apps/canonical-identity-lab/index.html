    Why this matters

    Closure needs stable objects.

    If the same structure acquired a new identity whenever a caller reordered JSON, censuses, cohorts, rankings and later-depth closure would depend on serialization accidents. Canonical identity removes that degree of freedom before scientific interpretation begins.
