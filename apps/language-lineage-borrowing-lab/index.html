08 / Reading discipline

Four layers, four meanings

CLASSIFICATION

Glottolog path

Published, attributed vertical grouping. It stays independently inspectable and replaceable by another published classification.

LEXICAL RECORD

WOLD form

One expert-curated form and borrowed-status annotation for one selected meaning.

BORROWING

Source relation

One donor-to-target-form record. It does not characterize the whole language and cannot become genealogy.

SIMILARITY

Display signal

A Unicode edit comparison only. It creates zero cognacy assertions and zero parent edges.
