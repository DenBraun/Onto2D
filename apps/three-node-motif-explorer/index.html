Reproduction contract

What was frozen before the run?

The case changes no rule after seeing the ranking. Raw source files are fetched and hash-verified rather than redistributed without a stated license.

  1. 01
    Graph convention

    Simple directed graph; 424 dictionary nodes; `target, source` columns reversed on import; unsigned edges; no self-loops.

  2. 02
    Occurrence rule

    Complete induced subgraphs on unordered triples of distinct nodes; weak connectivity; each triple counted once.

  3. 03
    Null model

    Per-node in-, out- and mutual-degree-preserving target swaps; 1,000 deterministic trials; mFinder-compatible sample deviation.

  4. 04
    Ranking test

    Descending finite Z-score. Primary agreement required 030T first and alone at empirical upper-tail P < 0.01.
