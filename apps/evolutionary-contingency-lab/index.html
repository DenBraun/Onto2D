The present was still Cit-.
The future was not equally open.

06 / What the result gives us

History changes the menu of reachable futures

SUPPORTED

Background matters

Under these experiments, later Ara-3 samples were more likely to yield Cit+ than the null model expected.

NOT DETERMINISTIC

History does not force the outcome

Many replays from later samples still produced no Cit+ observation. Potentiation changes propensity, not destiny.

CONDITIONED

The protocol is part of the question

Reachability here means “observed from this background under this exact replay,” not accessibility in every environment.

UNRESOLVED

Generation is not the mechanism

The selected tables do not identify a unique potentiating mutation, genotype, or clone-level causal chain.

07 / Historical Load

A strong candidate, not an invented scalar

LTEE is a priority candidate for a future empirical Historical Load study. The current release remains bounded: The published mean-generation shifts and P values quantify history-conditioned propensity; they are not substitutes for dH.
