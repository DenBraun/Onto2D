08 / Reading discipline

What this release refuses to infer

OBSERVED

Catalogue values

Reported measurements keep their uncertainties. They do not absorb downstream orbit or population meaning.

ESTIMATED

Parameters and orbits

Gaia Apsis and the paper companion table remain separately attributed derived layers.

CLASSIFIED

Rule profiles

The four cohort names are Onto2D selection rules, not native Gaia population labels.

COMPATIBLE

Historical context

Radial or counter-rotating patterns can fit several histories; they do not prove accretion.

UNKNOWN

Birth and ancestry

No birth site, common ancestry, or unique formation route is produced for any source.
