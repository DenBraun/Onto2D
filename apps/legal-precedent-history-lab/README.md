# Legal Precedent History Lab

This light-theme explorer verifies the exact Legal Precedent case artifact in
the browser before rendering it. The default graph is the official-date
context for `Green`; a full-record control reveals later opinions without
making them inputs to 1968.

Native `CITES` arrows, source-attributed treatment overlays, provider date
disagreements, binding-status unknowns, and counterfactual graph ablation are
visually separate. CourtListener citation counts remain display-only. The lab
does not determine current law and is not legal advice.
