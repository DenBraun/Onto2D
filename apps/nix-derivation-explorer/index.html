The useful distinction

Content answers “what did we get?” Construction answers “what exactly did we ask Nix to build?”

Use content identity for deduplication and delivery checks. Use derivation, closure, or environment identity when provenance, rebuildability, review scope, or supply-chain differences matter.

Evidence boundary

What was observed—and what was not

Nix instantiated every derivation and emitted the captured `.drv` records. Nix also materialized and inspected the fixed content object. Builders were not executed, so the input-addressed control has no observed output content.

  • Raw ATerm and derivation JSON are cross-checked field by field.
  • Direct inputs are native; transitive closure and environment profiles are labeled derived.
  • “Unresolved” means missing realization evidence, never inferred equality.
  • Historical Load is intentionally undefined: this case declares no finite admissible path space.
