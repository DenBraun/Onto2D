Evidence boundary

What was verified - and what was deliberately not claimed

Every manifest, config, and uncompressed tar layer is checked against its OCI descriptor. Filesystem states and Historical Load are derived projections over a deliberately tiny fixture profile.

  • Whiteouts delete lower-layer files and never appear as final files.
  • Native descriptor order stays distinct from derived state equality.
  • The reversed-order control has no native manifest and remains counterfactual.
  • This evaluator is not a registry client, image signature verifier, or general container runtime.
