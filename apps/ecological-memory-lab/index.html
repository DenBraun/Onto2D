11 / Reading discipline

What this release does not claim

MEASURED

Vegetation-height projection

Four quantiles summarize retained high-vegetation returns. They are not the complete ecosystem state.

RECORDED

Creek Fire context

Four source records and a published tutorial interpretation establish context, not a direct spatial causal join.

CHANGED

Measurement protocol

The 2019 and 2021 surveys use different sensor systems. Protocol identity is false by construction.

NOT ESTIMATED

Causal effect

No control tile, exact perimeter intersection, or adjustment design supports attribution to fire.

NOT OBSERVED

Recovery trajectory

Two survey dates do not establish a continuous path, a future state, or a recovery curve.
