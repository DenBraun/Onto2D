# SG2-041: prospective geometric added-value study v1

Frozen on 2026-09-07 before source generation and comparative measurements.
This is the bounded synthetic H4 evaluation task, not all of R7. SG2-042/043
provider/parameter robustness, R10/R11 domain validation and website gates remain.

## Population and leakage

Generate 32 distinct oriented, weakly connected graphs with six vertices and
eight arcs. For proposal counter 0 through 4095, SHA-256 of UTF-8
`onto2d-sg2-041-prospective-v1:<counter>` supplies its first 15 bytes. Visit the
unordered vertex pairs in lexicographic order; byte modulo three means absent,
forward or reverse arc. Accept exactly eight arcs and weak connectivity; reject
isomorphic duplicates by the minimum row-major off-diagonal adjacency bit string
over all 720 vertex permutations. Exclude six-vertex/eight-arc graph orbits in
the existing response-signature fixtures and geometric-signature source packs.
Stop at the first 32 accepted or fail at the proposal budget. Keep all proposal
dispositions. This deterministic, label-biased generator is not a uniform null.

Freeze sources, requests and the pair list before computing responses/geometry.
Each accepted orbit is one evaluation unit. One ID/order/presentation transport
belongs to that same unit and supplies an invariance pair, never another sample.
Use all 496 unordered pairs of distinct units as structural negatives; their
non-isomorphism is established independently of response/geometry outcomes.
The degree-matched subset is fixed by equal joint in/out-degree multisets before
measurement. Include all 32 invariant positives. No train set, fitting,
calibration, threshold selection or outcome-driven replacement is performed.

Four separately marked development sources reuse the known diamond-feedback,
isolated extension, feedback subdivision and three-node path constructions.
Their three pairs are feedback/isolate, feedback/subdivision and path/self.
They check interpretation and missingness, and never enter evaluation counts.
The evaluation panel is prospective and orbit-disjoint from those development
units and the specifically audited earlier fixture populations. This does not
claim a blinded external dataset or statistical independence of graph pairs.

## Fixed comparison domain

Use `topology-only-v1`, the unchanged three-family ResponseSignature-v0 and R6
categorical mismatch rule. All analyses use the entire verified source graph.
Geometry requests Forman `structural-geometry` with `unit-v1`, all unit Ollivier
edges at half idleness, and flow with unit initial lengths, half step, half
idleness, four iterations, tolerance 1/1000000, two stable transitions and strict
final-length cut at 2/1. These settings are fixed, not selected by a result.

Every unit must have the same request/profile conditions and exact agreement of
response and geometry node/edge populations. Only exact unit Forman point values
are admitted: certified non-point intervals, other providers, typed domains,
non-unit initialization, differing profiles/scopes and partial edge coverage do
not acquire comparison authority from matching fingerprints. No interval
midpoint, overlap-equality or pair-specific tolerance is used. Broader interval
comparison is outside this explicitly bounded domain.

A is the mean of three exact categorical response-family mismatches. B is the
mean of those three plus the three entire geometric-family mismatches. Static
A+curvature uses the first five coordinates. Weights and scales are all one;
fractions are reduced exactly. These distances describe family differences, not
their magnitude or a calibrated similarity. They are pseudometrics only on the
fixed complete domains: each categorical coordinate satisfies triangle, and
the fixed positive sum preserves it. No public R6 policy is modified.

Primary paired evaluation requires all response families and all three geometry
families on both sides, passed invariance, all edges and frames 0 through 4.
Any early stop leaves missing tail frames and makes that pair ineligible, even
at a fixed point. Preserve raw A coverage and all B omissions; use A on precisely
B's complete population for primary scores. Never pad, align by termination,
average available coordinates or silently remove a failed unit. Known missingness
is retained; malformed certificates or resource errors abort without a success
report. A failed run is a failed evaluation, never a replacement source.

## Outcomes, baselines and inference

The sole primary outcome is the paired change in discrimination of structural
negatives: fraction with exact distance greater than zero, B minus A on the same
eligible pairs. Report gained/lost/both/neither counts and exact denominators.
No mean-distance increase is interpreted as improvement. Appending coordinates
cannot reduce binary discrimination on a fixed complete domain; gaining a split
alone does not establish scientific utility. Report invariant false differences
and missing pairs separately. Zero eligible pairs means an indeterminate result,
not zero effect. Retain a zero gain without tuning this panel.

Secondary descriptive outcomes use the same primary population: A+static
curvature versus B, the predetermined degree-matched negative subset, and seven
baselines: node/edge counts; joint directed degrees; the seven topology summaries;
induced directed three-node motif census; exact adjacency traces of powers 1–6
(a spectral invariant); directed one-dimensional color refinement for six rounds;
exact directed canonical identity. For refinement, start with one color and
jointly rank exact [old color, sorted incoming colors, sorted outgoing colors]
tuples across the frozen unit panel at each round; compare all round histograms.
No collision-prone feature hashing replaces exact tuple equality. Typed motif
and semantic role baselines are unavailable because this panel declares neither
types nor domain roles. No numerical spectral eigensolver or learned kernel is
needed for this finite exact discrimination question.

Canonical identity is both the independent structural label and a ceiling
baseline, not a rival on semantic analogy. Motif, spectral and color baselines
are diagnostics; no best-performing secondary comparison is promoted to primary.
Report cases where B gains over A but simpler baselines already distinguish them.
All finite-panel pair outcomes are enumerated exactly. Pairs share source units;
there are no independent-pair confidence intervals, p-values, population
generalization, or multiple-comparison significance claims. Negative/indeterminate
findings constrain claims. Provider and flow sensitivity remain SG2-042/043.

## Evidence, cost and acceptance

Store each source-bound response and geometric artifact once per unit; suite
comparisons reference exact unit hashes. Verification reconstructs sources and
all response/geometry certificates, values, coverage, baselines, pairs and
aggregate arithmetic. A source-only Python reference supplies permutation truth,
matrix/motif/spectral/refinement baselines, existing independent graph responses
and geometric descriptors. Independently regenerate numerical transport/flow
with pinned NetworkX 3.2.1. Pin all source/protocol/helper hashes. Check exact R6
agreement, transport, missingness, source/profile/receipt mismatch, altered
denominators, rehashed forgeries, schemas and finite-domain metric laws.

Report deterministic work (response observations/targets, transport problems and
frames, baseline permutations, artifact bytes) for every unit. Separately write
optional local wall-clock measurements with runtime/platform and execution mode;
times are not canonical scientific values and certificate replay is not solver
generation. Cumulative stored unit output is bounded to 128 MiB, each unit to
32 MiB, suite to 8 MiB, at most 68 units and 531 pairs. All upstream limits remain.
Accept only after the independent checks, repository tests and full build pass.
