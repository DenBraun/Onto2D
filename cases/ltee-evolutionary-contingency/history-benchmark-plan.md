# LTEE — History Matters experimental contract draft

Status: CONTRACT_DRAFT. The existing source-locked replay evidence is available;
a benchmark evaluator and complete P/H/Y protocol are not yet frozen.

The candidate should compare Cit+ accessibility under each of the three exact
published Ara-3 replay protocols. Preserve their different sampling units,
generation sets, exposure and denominators. Do not pool mutant counts, sample
sizes or source-attributed P values into one experimental effect.

P must state which phenotype/environment information was available before each
replay. H adds the recorded source generation and any genuinely resolved sample
identity. Generation alone is not a complete genotype or a unique clone.
Y is protocol-specific replay observation, with non-observation preserved as
non-observation rather than impossibility. Original lineage history and newly
observed replay history remain distinct.

Required work: define the unit/cohort and cutoff for each protocol; freeze
eligibility, denominators, target and evaluator; state which P0 comparison the
published evidence actually supports; review any exchangeability assumptions
before adding a null; retain unresolved genotype/sample ambiguity. A protocol
without a defensible P0 contrast stays not evaluated.

This experimental candidate is not a causal intervention identification claim.
No P value is recomputed in the pilot, and no finite mutation path/cost space
exists here to justify Historical Load.
