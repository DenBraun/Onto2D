# Current roadmap

The project has no published first release. Implemented means an executable,
checked contract within its declared bounds, not a general scientific result.
The active development priority is Structural Geometry on DREAM4 and C. elegans.

The authoritative APIs and capability evidence live with the packages. Source
model versions do not certify scientific validity or announce a software release.

## Active sequence: Structural Geometry

The [research plan](structural-geometry/RESEARCH.md) owns the detailed gates.

1. Source acquisition is complete for 13 locked downloads: DREAM4, the
   C. elegans functional archive, eight anatomical graphs and author metadata.
   Native graph/observation adapters and neuron mappings are next.
2. Audit applicability, missing observations and graph size. Define bounded
   source scopes and a task-specific response/feature profile before scoring.
3. Freeze independent targets, baselines, grouped splits, metrics, costs and
   negative-result reporting. Public, already inspected data is not blinded
   preregistration.
4. Run the DREAM4 pilot, then the C. elegans functional study. Keep the
   developmental connectomes as a separate secondary study.
5. Evaluate provider sensitivity, robustness and cost on the accepted task.
   Publish findings and limitations regardless of direction.
6. Implement a Structural Geometry page over verified artifacts once source,
   protocol, replay and interpretation gates pass. A positive result is not a
   prerequisite; an honest reproducible result is.

Provider sweeps, persistence, higher-order models and additional domains are
conditional extensions. They do not precede a usable task and an informative
baseline. The two selected biological directions are not evidence across four
independent domains.

## Completion rules

A stage needs implementation, independent reference agreement where relevant,
source/coverage accounting, reproducible commands and current documentation.
Passing tests establishes the declared computational behavior. Source truth,
empirical usefulness and generality require their own evidence.

A failed hypothesis is a result. Retain the frozen study and revise the next
study explicitly; do not replace outcomes, suppress exclusions or tune the same
sample until the result becomes positive.
