## Homepage research map

Distinguishability is a compact foundation strip/root above the research map,
not a fifth peer card. Its question is “What can be told apart?” with a short
boundary: this is an analytical principle and Level-0 methodological motif,
not an empirically established physical law.

The map connects Canonical Identity (“What remains the same?”), Network Motifs
(“What recurs?”) and Historical Load (“Does the path add a distinction?”) to
Structural Geometry (“What is organized the same way?”). These are complementary
research questions; the map does not make Geometry the authority over their
existing evidence or identity semantics. Preserve access to each current study.

## Lab interaction

Keep a selected source fragment or pair through the sequence:

```text
OBSERVATION REGIME
Declared graph -> Shadow geometry -> Flow -> Response/geometric signatures -> Comparison
```

The source panel remains fixed while derived views change. Users can inspect
native source relations, explicit scope boundaries, metric/provider origin,
curvature, flow frames/termination, signature components and the comparison's
regime, coverage and unresolved observations. Layout coordinates are presentation
only. Flow animation moves through stored derived state and never edits source
weights or relations.

The selector offers only implemented compatible regimes. Unsupported history,
absent signatures or incompatible typed mappings produce an explanatory state,
not a fabricated metric. Changing regime can legitimately change a conclusion;
the current regime is visible beside every result. Separate exact
indistinguishability, positive distance/proximity and indeterminate status.
Do not render one unconditional “similarity percentage”.

Default display consumes frozen verified artifacts. Indicate what was verified:
source/policy binding, certificate/history replay and computational limits.
Never imply the browser reran the external optimizer when it only verified a
stored certificate. New calculations require an explicit bounded action and
an appropriate worker/adapter with progress, cancellation and failure display.
Do not rerun Python or long analyses merely because a page opened.

Provide keyboard-accessible regime/source controls, textual frame summaries,
pause/step/reduced-motion behavior and non-color-only status cues. Keep exact
identities and detailed diagnostics inspectable without flooding the primary
explanation. Mobile layouts retain source/derived distinction and comparison
coverage. UI tests must cover stale results after selection changes, unsupported
regimes, rejected artifacts and missing components.

## Candidate four-domain section

Suggested heading: “Different vocabulary. Candidate common organization.”

| Candidate | Native structure and initial probe questions | Required source boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Biological: homeostatic cellular regime | Regulatory/transport layers, nutrients and feedback; constraint/feedback/redundancy probes | Independently source a particular model; no universal homeostasis topology |
| Technological: orchestrated service regime | Replicas, balancing, control policy and health telemetry; policy/feedback/support probes | Preserve a native non-isomorphic representation; no biological-equivalence claim |
| Physical: driven convection pattern | Material, driver, gravity/boundary and transport feedback | Lock an explicit physical model and intervention interpretation before scientific scoring |
| Social: institutional role stabilization | Participants, demand, rules, coordination and enforcement | Keep rules/enforcement distinct and disclose evidence; no shared-equations inference |

The proposed “constrained feedback stabilization” family is a hypothesis. The
four hand-authored diagrams in the attachment are explanatory candidates, not
positive ground truth. Freeze independent native sources and role/probe mappings
before calculating comparison scores. Controls may switch native/abstract
vocabulary, graph/response/geometry/flow views and available regimes. They must
not secretly replace all four graphs with one common hand-drawn template.

No cross-domain PASS/FAIL or candidate proximity is shown as measured until
source-locked cases and the appropriate R11 benchmark exist. Even a supported
proximity does not establish semantic identity, common cause, shared equations
or common history. A method page can function without this results section.

## Explanatory bridges and evidence

The vision bridge, “Why start with distinction?”, may contrast
`features -> correspondence -> disparity -> geometry` with
`observables -> distinctions -> distance -> structural geometry`. It is an
analogy of representational order. The named vision article lacks an identifiable
source in the supplied material; do not invent attribution or claim validation.

Hamilton/Perelman comes after the actual graph algorithm, labeled “Analogy of
strategy — not the same mathematics.” Use flow cut, threshold partition or
structural decomposition; never present threshold cuts as Perelman surgery.
Bailey is contextual motivation for complementary geometry/topology/dynamics,
not confirmation of Onto2D. Fisher–Rao remains outside the default deterministic
graph profile. The [revision analysis](REVISION_ANALYSIS.md) records checked
research anchors and their limits.

## Results and falsification panel

Show which hypothesis was tested, source/probe/provider locks, sample and coverage,
the exact response-only and stronger graph baselines, null controls, evaluation
protocol and observed outcome. Distinguish computational reproduction from
scientific validation. Display negative, redundant, capped, failed and
indeterminate outcomes as first-class results. Decorative demonstrations and
recorded measurements require visibly different labels.

The implementation tasks are SGWEB2-001–007 in the roadmap. The full Lab follows
R11. A narrower method-under-test release after R0–R7 readiness would have its
scope stated explicitly; it could not claim the later stages are complete.
