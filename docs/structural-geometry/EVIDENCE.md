## Claim boundaries

The computational implementation establishes that the declared directed statistic was
computed reproducibly from an exact, verified source graph under a disclosed
unit metric. Synthetic controls and separate reference code check arithmetic
and interpretation of direction. Full-model results describe this catalogue
snapshot, including its source classifications and known findings.

It cannot establish causal validity, generative admissibility, scientific
significance, improved prediction, emergence, cross-domain equivalence or a
universal geometry. Negative curvature is not by itself a causal bottleneck;
positive curvature is not proof of a coherent mechanism. A source snapshot
passing verification is not an independently reviewed scientific dataset.

The Hamilton–Perelman analogy is research motivation. No smooth-manifold
theorem, Ricci-flow surgery or Poincaré result is implemented or inherited.
Curvature, metric evolution, topology and statistical inference require
separate definitions and tests. Multiple parents do not establish a joint
higher-order interaction; persistence alone does not establish significance.

Independent code is not independent scientific review. The Python reference
implementation is maintained in this repository and authored with the initial
implementation. No external reviewer approval is claimed. The computational
controls alone establish no empirical result; the separate
[DREAM4 pilot](../../cases/structural-geometry/dream4/README.md) and
[C. elegans study](../../cases/structural-geometry/celegans/README.md) report
response-ranking comparisons under the frozen biological protocol, including
the negative C. elegans result and its narrow eligible population. The separate
[D6.1 null comparison](../../cases/structural-geometry/robustness/README.md)
repeats the fixed learner on constrained rewired graphs with unchanged targets.
Its deterministic bounded sampler is not uniform; duplicates, insufficient
switches and unavailable comparisons remain explicit. Relative positions in
these indexed results are descriptive, not calibrated p-values.
The [D6.2 anatomy comparison](../../cases/structural-geometry/robustness/README.md#d62-adult-anatomy-sensitivity)
retains a different kind of result: Dataset8 has 8 eligible response groups /
37 pairs but fails the complete prepared-scope geometry gate at ALML. Its
shared subset with Dataset7 has only 4 groups / 18 pairs, below the fixed
study threshold. Source samples, target ranks and successful descriptors were
independently verified; no predictive fit was run or zero gain estimated for
these unavailable comparisons. A synthetic five-group control checks the
otherwise unused model path. Fixing a numerical bound alone cannot resolve
the insufficient shared population.

The [D6.3 graph-feature/capacity comparison](../../cases/structural-geometry/robustness/README.md#d63-graph-features-and-capacity-controls)
retains the primary targets, scopes and grouped learner while testing fixed
nonlinear B coordinates and expanded graph context. Geometry over expanded
context yields +0.0189 / +0.0089 / −0.1158 for DREAM4 knockout / knockdown /
Dataset7. Knockdown's conditional increase leaves both scores negative; Dataset7
improves with graph-only context and worsens when geometry is appended. All six
models and eight comparisons remain visible, without selecting the best control
after inspection. Equal coordinate count and tuning grid do not prove equal
statistical capacity. The reported nonconstant-column counts are not matrix
rank or effective degrees of freedom. Exact rational differences determine the
gain interpretation; binary64 summation tails cannot establish an improvement.
This is a secondary design after inspected primary results, with independently
checked arithmetic and models, not independent scientific validation.

The [D6.4 parameter comparison](../../cases/structural-geometry/robustness/README.md#d64-transport-metric-idleness-and-initialization)
retains five declared transport-metric/idleness/initialization variants and every
original scope and target. Static Forman remains unit; inverse target share uses
explicit synthetic unit analysis masses, not measured biological strengths.
Dataset7 remains below both respective graph-only baselines throughout this
panel. DREAM4 knockdown's original-baseline gain changes sign with the provider;
its expanded-geometry model's absolute scores remain negative. Parameter and
baseline dependence are findings, not permission to select a new primary after
scoring. Uniform scaling preserves normalized geometry and predictions exactly,
which verifies the computational normalization rule rather than biological
usefulness. Independent NetworkX checks successful flow trajectories; runtime
replay owns reproduction of declared arithmetic failures. One-factor settings
do not establish robustness over all parameters, their interactions or domains.

The [D6.5 scope/coverage comparison](../../cases/structural-geometry/robustness/README.md#d65-scope-selection-and-low-degree-coverage)
accounts for all 1,440 declared rooted selections in Dataset7/8. Parent degree
and mapping, stimulation, preparation and response eligibility are separate
coverage stages. Dataset7's five nodes with degree ≤5 all have prepared original
scopes, but none occurs in a primary target pair; predictive usefulness for
this stratum is untested. Dataset8's low-degree candidate pairs remain part of
its unavailable primary, not a new score. Incoming scopes retain 35 common
Dataset7 pairs in 6 groups. Both weak and incoming contexts refit all four
models on those rows: expanded geometry gain is +0.0897 and −0.0462 respectively,
while incoming gain over B is +0.0722. The weak-context expanded gain was −0.1158
on all 60 original pairs. Population, graph context and baseline therefore
affect the observed sign. Outgoing/two-hop comparisons lack sufficient common
groups. Independent reconstruction checks selections, matched ranks, 69 new flow
states, 1,316 descriptors, 2,160 distinct fits and 26,960 predictions. No scope
or subset becomes a replacement primary. The research stages D1–D6 are complete
with disclosed limitations. The [D7 laboratory](../../apps/structural-geometry-lab/README.md)
presents this evidence alongside the instrument controls. Its byte-pinned browser
payloads retain the original feature ablations, expanded baselines, every metric
and scope outcome, low-degree coverage and unavailable null/anatomy results.
Browser score replay is not biological retraining or independent validation.

The Ollivier stage additionally compares with the independently maintained
NetworkX network-simplex implementation. Our wrapper reconstructs neighborhoods
and distances and records the external code version/hash. Exact primal/dual
certificates establish mathematical optimality for the specified transport
problem. This is stronger computational evidence, still not independent
scientific review or validation of the chosen representation. A fragment result
describes its induced scope; it is not a full-model curvature value.

<a id="claim-boundaries--revised-observation-and-comparison-boundary"></a>

### Revised observation and comparison boundary

The [research program](RESEARCH.md) uses an inter-structure comparison
layer alongside existing intra-graph geometry. Distinguishability is a declared
analytical principle, not a physical law. Vision, Fisher–Rao, behavioural
pseudometrics and the topology literature are precedents or explanatory
analogies with different mathematical assumptions, not validation of Onto2D.

A complete zero pseudometric value means the chosen signatures are
indistinguishable under their shared regime. It does not imply common cause,
equations, mechanism, history, kernel identity or source-record equality.
Positive distance can still mean calibrated proximity under a separate benchmark
rule; it cannot be labeled exact indistinguishability. Missing mandatory history
or other evidence gives null/indeterminate, not zero. Partial coverage values
are not certified distances on one common pseudometric space.

Invariant scientific descriptors and exact provenance hashes have different
roles. Relabeling may preserve the former while changing the latter. Transparent
subdivision and bounded metric perturbations require explicit policies and are
not universal invariances. Observed graph deletion effects are not automatically
scientific interventions or kernel admissibility changes.

The [new hypothesis protocol](RESEARCH.md) requires comparison against
response-only and stronger graph baselines. Correct computations may yield no
added geometric information; retain that outcome. Cross-domain diagrams are explanatory analogies, not independent positive
ground truth. The selected empirical work is described in the research plan. Public measured proximity needs independently
sourced cases, frozen mapping and appropriate evaluation evidence.

## finite prospective response-versus-geometry evaluation

The [frozen protocol](../../cases/structural-geometry/added-value/PROTOCOL.md)
precedes sources and measurements. See the [results and commands](../../cases/structural-geometry/added-value/README.md).

<a id="finite-prospective-response-versus-geometry-evaluation--delivered-boundary"></a>

### Delivered boundary

This repository study consumes the unchanged ResponseSignature-v0,
StructuralPseudometric-v0 and GeometricSignature-v1 contracts. Its offline runner
reconstructs one full-source response and geometry artifact per unit, computes
baselines and binds separate pair/aggregate results. It adds no public distance
entrypoint, inferred vocabulary mapping or website behavior. The existing response
pseudometric and geometric descriptors preserve their exact contracts.

The data boundary is explicit: 32 prospective graph orbits, 32 corresponding
representation transports and four development sources. Source generation
rejects incorrect edge counts, disconnected graphs, duplicate orbits and the
specified earlier fixture orbits before any response/geometry scoring. The
generator, all 227 proposals and all 531 pair definitions are retained. Structural
truth is graph isomorphism, independently checked through all node permutations.
It supplies no semantic analogy labels. Transports stay grouped with their base
unit and development cases never enter primary counts. No fit/calibration split
is needed because there is no learned parameter or score-driven selection.

<a id="finite-prospective-response-versus-geometry-evaluation--fixed-conditions-and-arithmetic"></a>

### Fixed conditions and arithmetic

Every unit uses full-source `topology-only-v1` responses. Geometry uses unit
Forman, all unit Ollivier edges with half idleness, and mean-normalized flow with
unit initialization, half step/idleness, cap four, tolerance 1/1000000, two stable
transitions and a strict final-length cut at 2/1. Response and geometry source
contexts and exact populations must coincide within each unit. Across units,
the untyped feature/profile conditions must match. Raw source IDs and explicit
edge order never become comparison coordinates.

Only the exact unit profile is admitted. Weighted interval descriptors,
non-unit initialization, partial edge sets, typed regimes, arbitrary provider
contexts and differing flow policies do not become comparable through equal
fingerprints. This stage resolves their treatment by excluding them from its
bounded domain; it does not invent interval equality or semantic alignment.
Further studies must freeze their own conditions under the [research plan](RESEARCH.md).

Let `r1,r2,r3` be the complete mandatory response family values and `g1,g2,g3`
the complete geometric family values. For exact categorical mismatch `δ`,
weights and scales fixed to one give:

```text
A(x,y)      = (δr1 + δr2 + δr3) / 3
Static(x,y) = (δr1 + δr2 + δr3 + δg1 + δg2) / 5
B(x,y)      = (δr1 + δr2 + δr3 + δg1 + δg2 + δg3) / 6
```

Use actual exact values, with full within-family correlations and multiplicity.
Reduced integer fractions preserve zero exactly. The formulas describe which
families differ, not the magnitude of differences. Each categorical coordinate
obeys nonnegativity, symmetry, self-zero and triangle; a fixed positive sum
preserves these laws. Composing with a fixed complete feature map yields a
pseudometric on this study domain. It is not a metric on graph identities.

Primary comparison requires full B eligibility on both sides: complete response
families with passed invariance, both static curvature families and every flow
frame from iteration zero through four. Early fixed points do not fill missing
tail frames. Raw A/static distances retain their own availability, while primary
matched A/static/B are all null when B is unavailable. There is no pair-specific
omission, padding or mean over available features. Matching fractions outside
this fixed complete domain do not supply metric guarantees.

<a id="finite-prospective-response-versus-geometry-evaluation--outcomes-and-scientific-interpretation"></a>

### Outcomes and scientific interpretation

The primary outcome is the exact change in the fraction of structural negatives
with positive distance, on the same complete pairs. Record both/gained/lost/
neither cells. Appending categorical coordinates cannot lose a positive split
on a fixed complete domain; that mechanical monotonicity is not evidence of
scientific added value. Changes in mean distance are not an improvement score.

All 32 evaluation graphs have full geometric coverage; 21 have complete response
signatures. The resulting 210 eligible negative pairs are all distinguished by
A, static and B. Paired gain is zero. The 286 excluded pairs lack complete
responses, with no additional evaluation coverage loss due to geometry.
Joint directed degrees also distinguish all 210 pairs. Topology distinguishes
185, motifs 209, spectral traces 135, and refinement/canonical identity 210.
The panel has no degree-matched negatives; that subgroup is indeterminate, with
null rates. This is a disclosed ceiling, not evidence of superiority to strong
matched negatives. The frozen sources were not replaced after inspecting scores.

All 32 transports preserve measured values and baselines. Twenty-one have full
comparison coverage and eleven remain incomplete, with no observed false
differences. Development cases preserve early fixed-point tails and the known
response-only isolate collision without fabricating full B distances.

The seven baselines and secondary static-to-flow/subgroup outcomes are specified
before evaluation. Exact canonical identity is the structural-label ceiling.
Typed motif and semantic role baselines are unavailable because the panel has
no corresponding declarations. Every gain would be checked against the same
simple baselines. No best secondary result is promoted to primary.

This is a locally frozen prospective finite panel, not an external preregistration
or blinded domain study. Pairs share source units, so no independent-pair sampling
error, p-values or generalization claims are made. Zero gain constrains this
panel's claims and motivates separately designed harder populations; it does not
establish that geometry can never help. Biological evaluation and provider robustness remain separate studies in the
[research plan](RESEARCH.md).

### Evidence and verification

`geometric-added-value-unit.schema.json` and `geometric-added-value-suite.schema.json`
are separate closed contracts. A unit binds its expected source context, full
response/geometry receipts, baseline values, eligibility, work and unit hash.
The suite binds the frozen study/source/helper hashes, all 68 unit references,
531 pairs, raw/matched distances, coverage/reasons, baseline outcomes and exact
aggregate counts. It stores full unit evidence once, rather than copying it into
every pair. Timing is separate from canonical scientific values.

Verification repeats source generation and all response computation, verifies
external geometry certificates against expected sources/requests, recomputes
baselines, checks independent references and reconstructs every pair and total.
Rehashing altered values, profiles, eligibility, nested receipts, evidence links
or denominators cannot bypass expected-source replay. Output and expected source
snapshots remain immutable. Unit replay takes independently expected receipts;
their availability cannot be inferred from an untrusted artifact alone.

The independent numerical lock uses source-derived NetworkX 3.2.1 transport and
flow. A standard-library Python reference separately regenerates source orbits,
graph responses, geometric descriptors, exact baselines and all pair outcomes.
Its spectral calculation uses determinant coefficients and Newton identities;
the runtime multiplies adjacency matrices. All 64 three-node digraphs additionally
check seven baseline implementations, and all eligible base-unit triples check
the three fixed-domain distances. Existing response comparisons agree on representative
complete, invariant and incomplete pairs; every A value is independently checked.

Bounds are 68 units, 531 pairs, 32 MiB per unit, 128 MiB cumulative stored units,
8 MiB per suite and all upstream solver/source limits. All present units verified;
malformed data or resource errors abort rather than supply a partial successful
evaluation. Local generation/replay timings record their execution mode and do
not imply portable performance. External CI/review remain separate evidence.
