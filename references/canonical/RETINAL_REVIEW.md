# Optical/retinal pilot reconstruction

The subsequent [conditional-routing review](ROUTING_REVIEW.md) extends this
pilot without changing its historical-card dispositions.

Edition `2026.09.12.2` adds a scoped literature fragment to the Level-0
reconstruction. It internally reviews six historical cards, all seventeen of
their Evidence entries and all twenty-two incoming assertions. The
[decision ledger](retinal-review.json) preserves the complete original records,
per-field dispositions, study contexts, alternatives and revisiting tests.
The [policy](retinal-policy.json) is explicitly retrospective: source selection
and interpretation were not blind or preregistered.

The combined release has 44 entities, six rule specifications, 165 claims and
57 displayed connections: 48 descriptive connections and nine scoped
operating dependencies (`functional-support`). This layer records functional
support in the stated preparation. It is separate from the existing
formation-support vocabulary and does not certify an Onto2D closure or
assert generative emergence. The preceding `2026.09.12.1` edition used an
overly strong relation kind; its immutable source remains available for review. The original 249 cards and 971 parent assertions
remain accounted for. Internal review now covers 30 cards and 106 assertions;
219 cards and 865 assertions remain pending. Levels 3, 4 and 5 are only
partially reviewed. A boundary specialization does not complete the review of
its broad historical parent.

The pilot contains preparation-specific fragments. There is no adopted bridge
from the toad rod measurements to the macaque cone/midget pathway. Those are
different biological contexts. No empirical bridge from Level-0 CRT rules to
any retinal entity has been demonstrated. The record kinds describe phenomena,
structures, contexts and definitions; they are not newly admitted physical
CRT instances.

The sampling record is an explicit proposal to register optical input against
receptor positions. It has no paired dataset, fitted aperture map or tested
acuity prediction. Its dependencies therefore remain descriptive.

## Discriminating observations and limits

The broad necessity claim must distinguish **a response property** from
**the occurrence of a response**. In the studied macaque midget preparation,
HEPES removed surround/opponency reversibly while spike responses remained.
GABA/glycine blockade did not remove that opponency. The adopted edge concerns
tuning under those conditions; bath buffering is not a uniquely selective
molecular intervention. [Crook et al., Methods and Fig. 7](https://www.cns.nyu.edu/~tony/vns/readings/crook-etal-2011.pdf).

An additional input route is necessary in the representation: sampled mouse
melanopsin ganglion cells showed intrinsic light response and spike modulation
with rod/cone synaptic input blocked. This is a counterexample to a universal
requirement for that input, within the recorded cell selection.
[Do et al., Fig. 5 and Methods Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2794210/).

The recent structural work informs the pigment fragment, but does not validate
the project's proposed universal geometry. Its laser/crystal conditions and
possible multiphoton influence limit transfer to native single-photon events;
early structural change is not the later signalling state.
[Gruhl et al., Sec3 and Conclusions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10060157/).

These are retrospective literature contrasts, not new experiments or
independent raw-data reanalyses. The ledger compares the original coarse
claims, the scoped reconstruction and alternatives. Predictive ranking,
minimality and geometric advantage are marked **not tested**. A frozen target
dataset and evaluation policy are still required.
